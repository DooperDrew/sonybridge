Guided Setup

SonyBridge brings native Noise Canceling, 5-Band Hardware EQ, and Anti-Hands-Free audio routing to your Sony WH-1000XM3 on Windows 10/11.

1

Pair in Windows Bluetooth Settings

Ensure your WH-1000XM3 is paired with Windows. SonyBridge automatically discovers its MDR RFCOMM channel (UUID 96CC203E-...).

2

Automatic Stereo A2DP Protection

Whenever your headphones connect, SonyBridge prevents Windows from degrading audio into muffled Hands-Free telephony mode.

3

System Tray & 1-Click Profiles

Closing the window minimizes SonyBridge to the Windows System Tray so you can switch acoustic profiles anytime with one click.
